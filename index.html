02Services

Fixed scope. Fixed price, quoted after the call.

Every service below is fixed price, quoted after the call. Scope and duration are agreed in writing before we start.

  1. A.1

    LLM Security Review

    1 LLM feature or agent · 3 days

    Prompt injection (direct and indirect), data leakage, tool and permission abuse, and logging. Delivered as a report with prioritized remediation guidance.

  2. A.2

    LLM, RAG and Agent Red Team

    Up to 3 features or agents · 1 to 2 weeks

    The LLM Security Review scope applied to up to 3 features or agents, with adversarial testing of prompts, retrieval and tool use. Delivered as a report with prioritized remediation guidance.

  3. B.1

    AppSec Quick Assessment

    1 web app or API · 5 days

    Authenticated testing against the OWASP Top 10 and OWASP ASVS Level 1, covering access control, authentication, input handling and configuration.

  4. B.2

    DevSecOps Pipeline Gates

    1 repo or pipeline · 3 to 5 days

    SAST, dependency scanning, secrets detection, IaC and container scanning in your CI/CD, plus a short policy document for your team.

  5. B.3

    Cloud and Kubernetes Hardening

    1 AWS or Azure account, or 1 cluster · 5 days

    A CIS-based review of identity and access, network exposure and configuration, with a prioritized fix list.

  6. C.1

    RAG and Agent Discovery Sprint

    1 use case · 2 weeks

    A working prototype on your documents, an evaluation set, and a quoted build plan, with security designed in from the start.

Need the fixes implemented, or ongoing security help? Implementation and monthly support are available as separate, quoted engagements.

03How we work

Plain process, written down before we start.

  1. A 20-minute call

    To understand what you are shipping, your stack and your deadline.

  2. Written scope, fixed price

    Before any testing, we sign the scope and a testing authorization with you.

  3. Least-privilege access

    Time-boxed access. We are happy to sign your NDA, and we delete client data after delivery.

  4. A clear report

    What we found, why it matters, and what to fix first.

Our methods follow public standards: OWASP ASVS and WSTG for web apps and APIs, the OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications for AI features, and CIS Benchmarks for cloud and Kubernetes.

05Questions

Before you book.

Is a Snapshot a full penetration test?

No. It is one day of hands-on testing of one web app, API or LLM feature, within the scope of a signed authorization letter. Broader and deeper testing, like the work in our sample report, is part of a full LLM Security Review or AppSec Assessment.

What does the USD 500 cover, and what happens after?

One web app, API or LLM feature. One day of hands-on testing plus a written report with a threat model and every finding ranked by severity. It starts after a short scoping call and a signed authorization letter, with a report readout within about 3 weeks of scoping. USD 500, fully credited toward an LLM Security Review or AppSec Assessment. We delete client data after delivery.

What happens to our data?

We use least-privilege, time-boxed access and are happy to sign your NDA. We delete client data after delivery.

Do you implement the fixes?

Our reviews deliver prioritized remediation guidance. If you want the fixes implemented, or ongoing security help, implementation and monthly support are available as separate, quoted engagements.

Is the sample report from a real client?

No. It was produced on intentionally vulnerable demo apps on our own infrastructure: a local copy of OWASP Juice Shop and a deliberately vulnerable RAG chatbot we wrote for testing.

Can agencies work with you?

Yes, white-label. We work remotely in English and Portuguese.
