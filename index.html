How we work

  1. A 20-minute call to understand what you are shipping, your stack and your deadline.
  2. A written scope with a fixed price. Before any testing, we sign the scope and a testing authorization with you.
  3. Least-privilege, time-boxed access. We are happy to sign your NDA, and we delete client data after delivery.
  4. A clear report: what we found, why it matters, and what to fix first.

Our methods follow public standards: OWASP ASVS and WSTG for web apps and APIs, the OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications for AI features, and CIS Benchmarks for cloud and Kubernetes.
